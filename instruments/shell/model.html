Physical model

Suppose uniform thickness \begin{equation} \label{eq:model} \mu \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = D1 \frac{\partial^4 u}{\partial x^4}+D3 \frac{\partial^4 u}{\partial x^4}+(D2+D4) \frac{\partial^4 u}{\partial x^2\partial y^2}+ T_x \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+T_y \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} - 2R \mu \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} \end{equation} Plate dimension are \(L_x, L_y\) and thickness \(h\). Tension parameters \(T_x, T_y\) can serve as prestressing conditions, or encoding a curvature as for shells.
